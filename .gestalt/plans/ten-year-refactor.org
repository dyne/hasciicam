** DONE [#A] Move the live frame loop into an app_session module
:PROPERTIES:

:Change: Create src/app/app_session.h and src/app/app_session.c. Define hasciicam_session_start(), hasciicam_session_step(), and hasciicam_session_stop(). Move capture open/start/read/release/stop and grey buffer ownership there. Keep the first version synchronous and single-threaded.

** DONE [#B] Replace cross-module globals with context structs
